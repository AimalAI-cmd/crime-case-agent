
Title: The Zodiac Killer

Introduction

The Zodiac Killer was an unidentified serial killer who operated in Northern California in the late 1960s and early 1970s. The killer targeted young couples and individuals, and is known for sending taunting letters and cryptograms to the press and police, many of which remain unsolved. The case remains one of the most infamous unsolved mysteries in American criminal history.

Timeline

- December 20, 1968: First confirmed Zodiac attack, killing David Faraday and Betty Lou Jensen in Benicia, California.
- July 4, 1969: Second attack, killing Darlene Ferrin and injuring Michael Mageau in Vallejo, California.
- September 27, 1969: Third attack, killing Bryan Hartnell and Cecelia Shepard at Lake Berryessa.
- October 11, 1969: Fourth attack, killing Paul Stine in San Francisco.
- July 26, 1970: The Zodiac sends a letter to the San Francisco Chronicle, claiming responsibility for 37 murders.
- March 13, 1971: Last confirmed letter from the Zodiac is received.

Interesting Facts
- The Zodiac Killer's identity remains unknown despite numerous investigations and suspects over the years.
- The killer's cryptograms, including the 340-character cipher, were partially solved but some remain unsolved to this day.
