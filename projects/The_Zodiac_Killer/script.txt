
Title: The Zodiac Killer

Introduction

The Zodiac Killer was an unidentified serial killer who operated in Northern California in the late 1960s. The killer targeted couples and lone individuals, and is known for sending taunting letters and cryptograms to the press, some of which remain unsolved. The case remains one of the most infamous unsolved mysteries in American criminal history.

Timeline

- December 1968: First confirmed Zodiac attack, killing David Faraday and Betty Lou Jensen in Benicia.
- July 1969: Second attack, killing Darlene Ferrin and injuring Mike Mageau in Vallejo.
- September 1969: Third attack, killing Bryan Hartnell and Cecelia Shepard at Lake Berryessa.
- October 1969: Fourth attack, killing Paul Stine in San Francisco.
- 1969-1974: Zodiac sends a series of cryptic letters to local newspapers, claiming responsibility for the murders and taunting police.

Interesting Facts
- The Zodiac Killer's identity has never been confirmed, despite numerous suspects and investigations.
- The killer's cryptograms included a 340-character cipher that was finally solved in 2020, over 50 years after it was sent.
