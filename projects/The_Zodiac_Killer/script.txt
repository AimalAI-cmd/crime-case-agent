
Title: The Zodiac Killer

Introduction

The Zodiac Killer was an unidentified serial killer who operated in Northern California in the late 1960s and early 1970s. The killer targeted couples and lone individuals, and is known for sending taunting letters and cryptograms to the press and police, some of which remain unsolved. The case remains one of the most infamous unsolved mysteries in American criminal history.

Timeline

- December 20, 1968: First confirmed Zodiac attack, killing David Faraday and Betty Lou Jensen in Vallejo, California.
- July 4, 1969: Second attack, injuring Darlene Ferrin and killing Mike Mageau in Vallejo.
- September 27, 1969: Third attack, killing Bryan Hartnell and Cecelia Shepard at Lake Berryessa.
- October 11, 1969: Fourth attack, killing Paul Stine in San Francisco.
- July 26, 1970: Last confirmed letter sent by the Zodiac Killer.
- 1974: Last possible communication attributed to the Zodiac Killer.

Interesting Facts
- The Zodiac Killer claimed to have killed 37 victims, but only 5 confirmed deaths and 2 survivors are officially linked to him.
- The Zodiac's cryptograms included the 340-character cipher, which was finally solved in December 2020 after 51 years.
